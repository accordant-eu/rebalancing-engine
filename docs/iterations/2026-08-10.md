# Iteration Log: 2026-08-10

## Theme: Generalized Trade Optimizer Engine & US Tax Module (Oracle Assimilation)

### Overview
This iteration established a generalized, pluggable `TradeOptimizerInterface` across the engine and implemented Tranche 1 of the US Tax-Aware Optimization Module (assimilating Double Finance's `oracle` pattern).

### Key Accomplishments
1. **Architecture Decision Record**: Recorded [ADR-0058](file:///Users/johanhellman/Projects/rebalancing-engine/docs/decisions/0058-generalized-trade-optimizer-interface.md) defining `TradeOptimizerInterface` and `TradeOptimizerRegistry`.
2. **Layering & Separation of Concerns**: Decoupled trigger strategies (**WHEN**) from trade proposal generators (**WHAT**).
3. **Pluggable Trade Generators**:
   - `StandardRuleBasedTradeGenerator`: Wraps existing deterministic full-reset and boundary-targeting algorithms.
   - `TaxAwareUsTradeGenerator`: Pluggable US tax-aware optimizer contract validating `taxJurisdiction === 'US'`.
4. **API & CLI Surfaces**:
   - Exposed `optimizerType` and `tax_aware_us` in OpenAPI 3.0 specification.
   - Added `rebalance inspect optimizers` CLI subcommand to inspect registered trade optimizers.
5. **Comprehensive Test Suite**: Added unit and integration tests covering registry lookup, jurisdiction gating, and strategy resolution.
